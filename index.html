General Info
  • Art a hobby for me. Commissions usually take a week or more to complete.
  • I accept payment through Paypal.
  • I am open to work with characters that lack clear reference(s).
  • I don't have a strict defined style and am willing to experiment with you should you wish. See below for examples.
  • I draw:
    • Anthro characters.
    • Creatures.
    • Non-standard anatomy.
    • Robots/mechs.
    • and more. (if you don't see what you want, ask!)
Terms
  1. Payment is done half upfront, and half before the final artwork is delivered.
  2. Edits requests are allowed in the sketch phase, but are typically restricted after that.
  3. As artist, I hold the rights to the artwork. I may share it and work-in-progress(es) on this site as well as on other platforms.
    • Note: If you need to keep the artwork private, we can figure something out.
  4. As client, you agree not to:
    • Take credit for the artwork, or republish it without credit.
    • Modify or trace the artwork without permission.
    • Use the artwork for commercial purposes.
      (i.e. can't sell it, use it sell something, etc.)
    • Use the artwork to train, test or validate any generative AI program.
